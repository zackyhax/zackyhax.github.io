Ember Keep

Carry a dying flame through 29 dark rooms. Every step costs fire.

Play in your browser on itch.io

The game

A short turn-based puzzle game. You are a small robot with a torch, deep in a keep with no light. Every step burns one flame. Light a brazier to refill. Throw a spark to light what you cannot reach. Burn the oil, melt the ice, wade the water, and cross the bridge before it burns.

Moths fly at your flame. Sentries strike the tile they face. Count your steps, then reach the door.

Screenshots

Controls

Arrows or WASD to move, Space to wait, F then an arrow to throw a spark, Z to undo, R to restart, L to pick a room. + and − to zoom; drag, or Shift and an arrow, to look around. Or tap a tile next to you.
