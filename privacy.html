Privacy policy

For the app “zacky-yt-uploader”. Last updated 19 October 2026.

What the app is

zacky-yt-uploader is my private tool. I use it to upload videos to my own YouTube channel, Zacky Hax, and nowhere else. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

It asks Google for one permission: youtube.upload, to upload videos to my channel. It does not read, change or delete anything else in my account.

What it collects, stores and shares

It collects no personal data from anyone. The only thing it keeps is my channel’s own Google sign-in token, stored privately so my uploads can run. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

My app’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

My channel’s account can revoke the app’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
