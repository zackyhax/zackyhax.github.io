Privacy policy

For the apps “zacky-yt-uploader” and “Zacky Poster” (TikTok). Last updated 24 October 2026.

What the app is

zacky-yt-uploader is my private tool. I use it to upload videos to my own YouTube channel, Zacky Hax, and nowhere else. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

It asks Google for one permission: youtube.upload, to upload videos to my channel. It does not read, change or delete anything else in my account.

What it collects, stores and shares

It collects no personal data from anyone. The only thing it keeps is my channel’s own Google sign-in token, stored privately so my uploads can run. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

My app’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

TikTok: the app “Zacky Poster”

Zacky Poster is my private tool for posting my own short videos to my own TikTok account, @zackyhax, and nowhere else. It is not offered to anyone else.

It asks TikTok for two permissions: user.info.basic, to know which account it is posting to, and video.publish, to post my videos. It does not read anyone else’s data, comments or messages.

It collects no personal data from anyone. It keeps only my account’s own TikTok sign-in token, stored privately so my posts can run. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. My account can revoke the app’s access at any time in TikTok’s settings, under Security, Manage app permissions.

Removing access

My channel’s account can revoke the app’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
